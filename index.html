Where to Watch

Gridiron Guide shows known TV networks and streaming providers for NFL games. Tap Watch to open a supported provider app or website when available.

Provider access may require a separate subscription, TV package, or account.

Local TV Market

Local CBS and FOX availability can depend on your TV market. Open Settings → Location & DIRECTV and either:

  • Enter your 5-digit ZIP code, or
  • Tap Use location to estimate your market.

Scores & Refreshing

Gridiron Guide updates live game status automatically. You can also tap Refresh to request the latest current-week game data.

Final-game information may be cached on your device to improve startup speed.

Provider Links

Gridiron Guide does not host or stream games. Watch links open legitimate third-party provider apps or websites. Availability and deep-link behavior can vary by device, provider, account, and region.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Gridiron Guide stream NFL games?

No. Gridiron Guide is a schedule, score, and viewing guide. It helps you identify where a game is available and can open supported third-party apps or websites.

Why does a game show a different local network than I expected?

Local broadcast availability can vary by market. Confirm your ZIP code or use the optional location feature in Settings to identify your local TV market.

Why did a Watch button open a provider homepage instead of the exact game?

Some providers support direct game links while others only expose a general app or website destination. Gridiron Guide uses the most specific supported link available and may fall back to the provider's main destination.

Why are fantasy leaders unavailable for a game?

Fantasy leader information depends on player statistics returned by the game-data provider. It is shown for live and final games when the required statistics are available.

Does Gridiron Guide require location access?

No. Location access is optional and only requested after you choose Use location in Settings. You can enter a ZIP code instead.

What should I include in a support request?

Please include your device model, iOS version, Gridiron Guide version, the game or provider involved, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot can also be helpful.
